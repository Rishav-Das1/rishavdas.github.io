About Me

I'm a fifth-year Integrated M.Sc. student in Physical Sciences at the National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER), Bhubaneswar — an OCC of HBNI, Mumbai. My work spans astrophysics, computational science, and machine learning: from building automated pipelines that mine ALMA radio telescope archives, to developing graph neural network algorithms that have been peer-reviewed and published.

I'm drawn to problems that sit at the boundary between disciplines — where a physics intuition and a computer science toolkit have to work together. Outside of research, I enjoy reading about quantum computing, contributing to open-source scientific software, and occasionally gazing at the night sky with a dish antenna. I'm actively looking ahead to PhD opportunities in astrophysics, computational physics, or ML for science.

Experience & Education

  1. May 2025 — Present

    MSc Thesis — DRiveMiner

    NISER Bhubaneswar · Supervisors: Dr. Liton Majumdar & Dr. Nishikanta Khandai

    Building DRiveMiner: an automated CASA-based pipeline for protoplanetary disk analysis of ALMA radio telescope data. It uses nested sampling, self-calibration, and a novel non-parametric signal masking algorithm, plus a custom non-LTE radiative transfer model (integrating pythonradex) to compute molecular abundances at scale. Successfully constrained HCN and DCN in HD163296.

  2. May 2024 — Oct 2025

    Research Internship — LIMO  Published

    NISER Bhubaneswar · Supervisor: Dr. Subhankar Mishra

    Developed LIMO (Label Informativeness-Based Minority Oversampling), a novel graph oversampling algorithm addressing class imbalance in GNNs. Proved theoretically that GNN effectiveness is proportional to label informativeness via mutual information. Validated on homophilous and heterophilous benchmark datasets. Code open-sourced at github.com/smlab-niser/limo.
